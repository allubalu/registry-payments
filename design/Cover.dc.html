Property registration · statutory fee collection

Paying the state, correctly.

A citizen files a property transfer, sees exactly how the duty was computed in their own jurisdiction and currency, and pays through the gateway that market actually uses. A clerk approves or rejects. Every movement of money is recorded once, and only once.
