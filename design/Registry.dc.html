Every pack is marked synthetic, so citizens see a banner and receipts carry a disclaimer. A pack with unmarked rates will not load.

Live vs loaded

Five live packs are payable end to end — India, England, Japan, Dubai and California. Singapore is loaded: validated and computed, not yet offered. Promotion was one config line.

Checked at startup

Every pack's currency must be chargeable by its routed gateway. A mismatch stops the service starting rather than failing at checkout.
